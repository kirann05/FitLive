# 90-second web demo

1. Sign in. Choose **Explore demo**; point out the demo badge.
2. Today shows reduced recovery. Open **Why this?** and explain multiple signals and baseline confidence.
3. Open Train, start a session, add three sets and save. Progress and the next action update.
4. Open Eat, choose a sample food, confirm grams, link its matching pantry item and save.
5. Open Pantry to show the deduction, then Groceries to refresh and export a shopping list.
6. Ask the rules-based coach why the plan changed; it uses saved state and discloses that no AI provider is connected.
7. Open Progress to show recorded outcomes and the audit trail.
8. Show export/delete controls and clearly state native/AI release gaps.

Do not describe this demo as live HealthKit, AI inference, real grocery pricing, or completed native functionality.
